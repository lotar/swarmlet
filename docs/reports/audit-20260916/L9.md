### 1.6 — Raw-data defect: the `ctx4096 p16` cell's TTFT is not a TTFT

All 16 requests in `ctx4096 p16` have `total_ms − ttft_ms ∈ {1,2,3,4}` ms. `ctxconc.py:71` sets

**Verdict: flaky measurement, undocumented.** 1 of 8 cells (12.5%) is affected.
