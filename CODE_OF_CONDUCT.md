# Community code of conduct

We want technical disagreement to produce better evidence, not personal conflict.

Participants must:

- be respectful and specific;
- critique claims, code, tests, and evidence rather than people;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- protect private data and responsibly report security issues;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these rules. Serious or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@swarmlet.ai` with subject `CONDUCT`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers involved in a complaint must not decide its outcome alone.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, review systems, community spaces, and public events where someone represents the project.
